# Recommended Starting Target (similar to Mogul approach)

## Top vertical to attack first: **HVAC**

### Why HVAC first

1. **High frequency of operational pain** around the lead → job → payment workflow.
2. **Clear willingness to pay** for tools that reduce missed leads, no-shows, and truck-roll inefficiency.
3. **Fragmented software landscape** – many shops still run on a mix of spreadsheets, paper, and basic dispatch software.
4. **Seasonal pressure** creates urgency (summer AC, winter heat).
5. Strong presence of owner-operators who talk publicly on Reddit, Facebook groups, and industry forums.

### Concrete problem areas to seed first

| Workflow stage | High-signal problems to collect |
|----------------|---------------------------------|
| Lead response | Missed calls after hours, slow callback, lead source tracking |
| Estimating | Follow-up on open estimates, inconsistent pricing |
| Scheduling | No-shows, last-minute cancellations, tech routing |
| Job execution | Parts availability on truck, tech time waste in morning |
| Invoicing & payment | Slow collections, incomplete invoices, financing friction |

### Suggested first seed set (target 30–50 problems)

Focus on observed complaints from:
- r/HVAC, r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur
- Facebook HVAC owner groups
- ServiceTitan / Jobber community forums
- Google Reviews that mention operational pain (not just price)

### Success criterion for this vertical

If, after seeding and soft outreach to 20–30 HVAC owners, you see:
- Multiple "I have this problem too" on the same problems
- At least a few "I'd test" or "I'd pay" signals

…then expand to Plumbing and Electrical using the same playbook.
