# Business Solution Board

## Project Concept

Business Solution Board is a structured community where business owners can post real business problems and other business owners, operators, builders, developers, or members of the public can propose solutions.

The goal is not to become another general-purpose social network. The core product is a structured database of **business problems, proposed solutions, validation, and willingness to test or pay**.

## Core Loop

1. A business owner posts a problem.
2. Other businesses click **I Have This Problem Too**.
3. Members propose solutions.
4. Businesses indicate **I'd Test This**.
5. Businesses indicate **I'd Pay for This**.
6. Builders can claim a problem and work on a solution.
7. Results and successful solutions are recorded.

## Initial Target Market

Start with **home-service businesses**:

- HVAC
- Plumbing
- Electrical
- Appliance repair
- Garage doors
- Pest control
- Cleaning
- Roofing
- Landscaping

### Initial Problem Area

Investigate the workflow:

**Lead received → customer contacted → estimate → scheduling → job → invoice → payment**

Potential problems include missed calls, slow lead response, estimate follow-up, scheduling, cancellations, no-shows, dispatch, technician routing, customer updates, parts availability, invoicing, and collections.

## MVP

1. Post a problem
2. I Have This Problem Too
3. Propose a solution
4. Follow a problem
5. I'd Test This
6. I'd Pay for This
7. Categories
8. Search

Avoid initially building complex profiles, messaging, a large social feed, advertising, or unnecessary AI features.

## User Types

### Business Owner
Can submit and validate business problems.

### Solver
Can propose practical solutions.

### Builder
Can claim a problem and build a solution.

### Observer
Can follow and participate without claiming to be a business owner.

## Example

### Problem

> My technicians waste 30–45 minutes every morning figuring out which jobs have the right parts available.

### Validation

- 43 businesses have this problem
- 12 would test a solution
- 7 would pay for a solution

### Proposed Solutions

- QR-based truck inventory
- Better inventory software
- Automated parts tracking
- Outsourced inventory management

## Relationship to Mogul

Business Solution Board and Mogul can eventually complement each other.

**Mogul asks:**

> What boring business opportunity should I pursue?

**Business Solution Board asks:**

> What problems are businesses repeatedly experiencing, and who is willing to pay for a solution?

A future Mogul scout could use Business Solution Board data to identify validated opportunities instead of relying exclusively on external complaints and market research.

## Critical Risk

The biggest early risk is the empty-room problem:

> Nobody posts because nobody answers, and nobody answers because nobody posts.

The MVP should therefore be seeded with real business problems before attempting to build a large community.

The first validation question should be:

> Do people naturally confirm problems, propose solutions, and express willingness to test or pay?

## Research Strategy

Use public discussions to discover unsolicited complaints rather than repeatedly asking people generic questions about their pain points.

Useful sources include:

- Reddit small-business communities
- Industry-specific Reddit communities
- Alignable
- Facebook groups
- Industry forums
- Google search
- Google Trends
- Google Reviews
- BBB complaints
- Public business-owner discussions
- Industry podcasts and comments

The strongest signals are concrete complaints such as:

> "I spent my entire Saturday doing this manually."

rather than:

> "What business problems do you have?"

The first is an observed problem. The second is a survey response.

## Working Principle

**Don't build another place for businesses to talk.**

Build a place where business problems become:

**identified → validated → solved → tested → commercialized.**
