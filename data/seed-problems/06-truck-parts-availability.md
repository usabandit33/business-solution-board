### Technicians waste time figuring out parts on the truck

**Source:** Industry truck-stock guides + owner/tech discussions; concept example mirrored in real complaints  
**Vertical:** HVAC  
**Workflow stage:** Job execution / Parts  

> Technicians frequently report morning time spent checking what is actually on the truck versus what the schedule requires. Stock drifts because parts are used without logging. Shops aim for 85–90% first-visit completion but fall short when common parts are missing.

**Notes:** Directly matches the example problem in the project concept. Inventory apps and QR systems are emerging responses.
