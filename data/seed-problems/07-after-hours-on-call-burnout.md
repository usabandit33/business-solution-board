### After-hours / on-call burnout for owners and techs

**Source:** r/hvacadvice “How do you handle after hours heating and cooling calls without burning out?” (2025)  
**Vertical:** HVAC  
**Workflow stage:** Lead response / Operations  

> “Running a small HVAC shop, the toughest part lately has been handling after hours calls. Some nights it’s actual emergencies… other nights it’s just normal inquiry or scheduling needs. … remaining on call myself → there’s no work life balance.”

> “No techs will do a proper evaluation at 1:00am anyway, so not only is your tech & your techs family annoyed, you’re also doing a mediocre job for your customer.”

**Notes:** Strong signal. Many small shops still rely on the owner’s cell.
