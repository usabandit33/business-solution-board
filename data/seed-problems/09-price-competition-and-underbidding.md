### Racing to the bottom on price / underbidding by unlicensed operators

**Source:** Multiple r/HVAC and r/hvacadvice threads  
**Vertical:** HVAC  
**Workflow stage:** Sales / Pricing  

> “I bid jobs on the cheap side… I occasionally get underbid by unlicensed, hacks by thousands of dollars. Like literally people doing the job for $1,000 over the cost of the equipment.”

> “The first killer is racing to the bottom on price… You’ll bleed out in 18 months.”

**Notes:** Persistent structural problem. Many successful owners refuse to compete on price.
