### Administrative work consuming owner time (quotes, invoices, permits)

**Source:** HVAC Industry Pulse survey data referenced in 2025 analyses  
**Vertical:** HVAC  
**Workflow stage:** Operations / Admin  

> “Small HVAC companies are 6.5 times more likely than larger companies to cite administrative work as their primary challenge… Companies with 1-10 employees average 15.4 administrative hours per week… nearly $100,000 in lost revenue each year.”

**Notes:** Quantified pain. Quotes, invoicing, equipment registration, and permits dominate.
