### Cash flow swings and customers who don’t pay

**Source:** r/HVAC owner threads 2023–2025; “Pay me!” and stiffed-invoice posts  
**Vertical:** HVAC  
**Workflow stage:** Invoice → Payment  

> “I’ll have swings of $60,000 a month on average and I need to remember that in any time I could be paying out a ton of cash for weeks without seeing anything come in… I feel like I lost my concept of if any money is actually mine or just filters through me.”

> “I’ve had 2 DOCTORS stiff me on repairs the last 4 months $1000+ material cost both times… Told to leave an invoice… Both times got stiffed.”

> “Don’t bill, front, or allow lines of credit… You’ll have enough to worry about as is without worrying about collecting.”

**Notes:** Strong observed pain. Many owners now demand deposits or payment at completion.
