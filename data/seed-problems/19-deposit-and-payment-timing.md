### Taking jobs without proper deposits / Net-30 cash-flow traps

**Source:** Owner advice threads  
**Vertical:** HVAC  
**Workflow stage:** Invoice → Payment  

> “They land a big job, buy all the materials on credit, finish the work and the customer pays Net 30. Meanwhile rent is due now… My rule was simple, customer deposits fund the job, period. 50% upfront minimum…”

**Notes:** Direct cause of many business failures.
