### Missed calls after hours and during peak season

**Source:** Multiple Reddit threads (r/HVAC, r/hvacpeople, r/hvacadvice) 2024–2026; industry analyses citing 27–50% unanswered calls in peak season  
**Vertical:** HVAC  
**Workflow stage:** Lead response  

> “I used to run into this problem all the time: I’d be under a house or up in an attic, phone ringing, no way to answer. By the time I’d call back, the lead had already booked someone else. Missed calls are lost jobs.”

> “HVAC companies: 35-50% of calls go unanswered during peak season… roughly 80% of callers who hit voicemail don’t leave a message. They just move on to the next business on Google.”

**Notes:** Extremely high-signal, repeatedly confirmed. Owners report thousands in lost monthly revenue. Common workarounds (voicemail, answering services, personal cell) all have clear drawbacks.
