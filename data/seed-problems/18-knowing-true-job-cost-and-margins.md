### Not knowing true cost per hour or job profitability

**Source:** Long-time owner retrospective posts (r/hvacpeople)  
**Vertical:** HVAC  
**Workflow stage:** Pricing / Finance  

> “Most small HVAC owners have no idea what it actually costs them to run per hour. They throw a price at a job based on gut feeling… Your pricing needs to support 40% direct job costs, 30% overhead, 20% actual profit, and 10% buffer…”

**Notes:** Fundamental business literacy gap that leads to slow failure even when busy.
