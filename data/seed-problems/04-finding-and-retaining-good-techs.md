### Finding and retaining quality technicians

**Source:** Multiple r/HVAC and industry posts 2023–2026  
**Vertical:** HVAC  
**Workflow stage:** Operations / Hiring  

> “Finding good technicians and/or installers And retaining them is by far the biggest challenge I’ve had in the 10 years that I’ve been in business.”

> “Finding good employees, kids who actually want to work for their money.”

> “Skilled labor is becoming increasingly difficult to secure, and the competition for experienced employees is driving payroll higher across the country.”

**Notes:** Near-universal complaint. On-call expectations and burnout accelerate turnover.
