### Matching staffing to extreme seasonal swings

**Source:** r/HVAC thread “As an HVAC Business owner… What are your biggest problems” (2024)  
**Vertical:** HVAC  
**Workflow stage:** Scheduling / Operations  

> “I’m swamped in the summer, dead in October, January and March. I need to keep everyone busy when slow and keep them from burning out when we’re busy. Trying to match the work load with the amount of employees can be tricky.”

**Notes:** Classic feast-or-famine problem. Cash flow and retention both suffer.
