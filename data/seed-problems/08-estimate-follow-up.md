### Estimates go cold with no structured follow-up

**Source:** Industry analyses and owner discussions (2025–2026)  
**Vertical:** HVAC  
**Workflow stage:** Estimate  

> Owners report large numbers of open estimates that never close because follow-up is manual or forgotten. One analysis estimated recoverable revenue in the hundreds of thousands from better estimate sequences alone.

**Notes:** Classic leak in the funnel. High willingness-to-pay potential for simple automation.
