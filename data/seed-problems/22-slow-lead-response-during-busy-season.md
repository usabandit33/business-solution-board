### Leads slip away because response is too slow

**Source:** Multiple analyses and Reddit posts 2025–2026  
**Vertical:** HVAC  
**Workflow stage:** Lead response  

> “you can spend more on marketing and bring in more leads, but if calls are missed after hours, follow-ups wait until the next morning, or appointments take too long to book, those leads can still slip away. lost revenue isn’t a leads problem. it’s a response-time problem.”

**Notes:** Quantified in several posts as thousands of dollars per week for mid-size shops.
